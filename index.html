Good To Know

How do I order?

Message us on WhatsApp with what you would like. We will share the rate and details and confirm your order there.

Can I customise my chocolates?

Yes. Every order is prepared to your requirement, including the cocoa percentage, box and hamper.

How long do the chocolates stay fresh?

They have no preservatives and stay fresh for about 15 days at room temperature. Please store them in a cool place.

Do you deliver?

Currently we deliver within Rajkot. Delivery details are confirmed on WhatsApp.

Do you take bulk or corporate orders?

Yes, with custom packaging and logo branding. The minimum is about 50 boxes.
